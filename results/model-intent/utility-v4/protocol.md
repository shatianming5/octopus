# Utility v4 inline-intent mechanism protocol

This experiment isolates one authored mechanism claim: a necessary normal reducer
output can expose the same checkpoint choice early enough to improve native KV
retention without a separate inference request. It is not a natural-contention
pilot and cannot establish generalized serving utility.

The held-out matrix is fixed before model outcomes at seeds 8101 and 8202. Each
seed contains six wholly new semantic families with two opposed goals per family,
for 12 episodes. The three methods are APC, structural B6, and inline model
intent. They use identical weights, prompts, tuple carrier, candidate order,
2048-token candidate prefixes, 2048-token pressure, capacity, jobs, and physical
GPU. A separate four-episode smoke uses two different families and is not a
matrix arm.

Each episode constructs two equal candidate prefixes and a Brain root. Two
authored evidence commits enter the real reducer `start_commit` path. The normal
body is `{"dispatch_index": 0|1}` and that field, not a private label, dispatches
the actual cached follow-up. The runtime wraps the body as
`[0|1, normal_object]`. Pressure is submitted after exactly four normal-output
engine steps in every arm. The inline choice must already be observed, the body
must still be incomplete, and native victim selection happens only afterward.
APC and structural parse the same carrier but ignore its hint.

Capacity is fixed at 321 blocks including the null block: two candidate prefixes
use 256 blocks and 64 additional usable blocks cover the active normal request.
The pressure request therefore forces candidate-cache eviction. This fixed
causal pressure is authored for mechanism isolation and is not described as
natural multi-branch contention.

All native committed prefill and decode are counted for root construction, both
candidates, the normal reducer, pressure, and continuation. Counters must
reconcile with scheduled ranges. Foreground latency spans episode start through
the body-dispatched continuation. Background latency starts at the fixed pressure
submission. Policy and adapter CPU time, normal choice-ready time, normal
completion time, candidate residency, weights, capacity, and GPU UUID are
recorded.

Every seed must independently satisfy all gates: at least 11/12 correct inline
choices; at least 80% of available gross continuation saving; at least 10% total
native prefill reduction versus structural; at least 70% net oracle saving;
foreground mean no slower than structural; background P95 no more than 5% above
APC; no background work increase; at least 10/12 exact final answers in every
method; and zero paired inline quality regression. A valid failure is terminal
`SCIENTIFIC_NO_GO`. Instrumentation failures require repair and rerun of the
same frozen protocol.

The tokenizer freeze must establish no more than 100 incremental control tokens
per episode or 1200 per trace, no more than four carrier-envelope tokens per
episode, and no more than 1024 tokens for the longest normal prompt plus output.
The complete smoke plus six matrix traces has a hard 30 GPU-minute budget on one
physical GPU, with no retries, added seeds, training, pilot, or formal route.
